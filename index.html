Club constitution · abridged
Article I. Attendance & absence.

Attendance is at the member’s sole discretion. No attendance register shall be kept. Silence shall not constitute a breach, incur a fee, or trigger follow-up correspondence. Membership may be suspended or retired on request, without explanation.

Article II. Currency & jurisdiction.

CNP is a fictional, non-transferable unit with no monetary value. The opening 24 points and 30-point threshold await ratification. Personal availability, replies, and private matters fall outside committee jurisdiction. All amendments and benefits require mutual agreement.

Article III. A discreet establishment.

This app has no analytics, accounts, location access, or shared database. Notes stay in this browser when storage is available. They do not sync or reach the other person unless you choose Share or copy them. Hosting providers may retain ordinary access logs. Anyone with access to this device can read its saved notes.
